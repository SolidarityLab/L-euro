# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in L-euro. The specification is a **draft for
comment**, and all feedback is welcome.

## How to contribute

- **Questions and comments:** open an issue. Bulgarian or English is fine.
- **Changes to the specification:** open a pull request against `main`
  touching `spec/`. Explain the reason and, where possible, the use case.
- **Schemas and examples:** pull requests against `schemas/` or `examples/`.
  Examples MUST validate against the schema.
- **Implementations:** tell us in an issue labelled `implementation`, so you
  can be counted for the Implementers' Council (see GOVERNANCE.md).

## Language

The specification is written in English. The position paper is in Bulgarian;
an English version is planned.

## Licensing of contributions

By contributing you agree that:

- text contributions are licensed under CC BY 4.0;
- schema, example and code contributions are licensed under EUPL 1.2.

We use the Developer Certificate of Origin (https://developercertificate.org/):
sign off your commits with `git commit -s`.

## Conduct

Be respectful and assume good faith. The maintainer may hide or lock
discussions that are abusive. The project follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Reports: office@solidaritylab.eu.
