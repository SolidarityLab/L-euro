# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

DTEP-S is a protocol specification project addressing real-world problems in humanitarian and social protection contexts. Contributors include protocol architects, implementers, domain experts from humanitarian organisations, and institutional stakeholders.

We are committed to maintaining a collaborative environment where contributions are evaluated on technical and substantive merit, and where all participants are treated with respect.

---

## Standards

**Expected behaviour:**

- Engage with technical arguments on their merits
- Provide constructive feedback with specific references to specification text
- Acknowledge uncertainty and disagreement openly
- Respect the operational and institutional context that motivates design decisions
- Use clear, precise language — this is a specification project

**Unacceptable behaviour:**

- Personal attacks or disparagement of contributors
- Dismissing domain expertise (humanitarian operations, social protection administration) as irrelevant to technical decisions
- Deliberate misrepresentation of specification content
- Harassment of any form

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, and any official communication channels associated with DTEP-S.

---

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to **office@solidaritylab.eu**.

Reports will be reviewed by the Lead Maintainer. Responses may include a warning, temporary suspension from contribution, or permanent exclusion from the project depending on severity.
